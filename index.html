The studio

Original sculpts, made to be printed.

DRAKO is a digital-first figure studio. We design, sculpt and prepare our own characters and display pieces, then sell the printable files directly to makers and collectors.

Every release is built around a complete scene: a character, interchangeable accessories and a display base. Files are exported at a defined scale in millimeters and prepared in supported and unsupported versions, so the buyer can print with the workflow they already use.

Our first collection, Diosas del Rock, explores heavy metal, fantasy and 80s/90s imagery through original characters. Releases are announced through our public channels and fulfilled digitally: no physical inventory, no shipping.

We work with personal-use and commercial licenses. Commercial buyers can sell physical prints; the digital files themselves are never resold or redistributed.

DRAKO figures LLC creates and sells original digital sculpts for 3D printing. Revenue comes from direct sales of digital files, collection releases and commercial licenses. There is no physical inventory and no shipping operation.

Products are delivered as downloadable files. Buyers print them on their own equipment. The company operates online and serves individual makers, painters and small print shops.

Brand and studio: DRAKO figures. DRAKO is a standalone brand with its own catalog, pricing and audience.
