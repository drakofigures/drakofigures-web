The studio

Original sculpts, made to be printed.

DRAKO is a digital-first figure studio. We design, sculpt and prepare our own characters and display pieces, then sell the printable files directly to makers and collectors.

Every release is built around a complete scene: a character, interchangeable accessories and a display base. Files are exported at a defined scale in millimeters and prepared in supported and unsupported versions, so the buyer can print with the workflow they already use.

Our current release is Goddess of Heavy Metal, the first of four figures in the Goddess of Rock collection. The remaining three are in development. The line explores heavy metal, fantasy and 80s/90s imagery, and every release is announced through our public channels.

We work with personal-use and commercial licenses. Commercial buyers can sell physical prints; the digital files themselves are never resold or redistributed.

DRAKO figures LLC creates and sells original digital sculpts for 3D printing. Revenue comes from direct sales of digital files, collection releases and commercial licenses. There is no physical inventory and no shipping operation.

Products are delivered as downloadable files. Buyers print them on their own equipment. The company operates online and serves individual makers, painters and small print shops.

Brand and studio: DRAKO figures. DRAKO is a standalone brand with its own catalog, pricing and audience.

Policies

Delivery, refunds & license

Clear terms for customers, platforms and payment partners.

Digital delivery

All products are digital files (STL). After payment, the buyer receives a download link by email or through the sales platform. No physical item is shipped.

Files are delivered in the format and scale stated on each listing.

Refunds

Because files are digital and delivered immediately, sales are final once the download is available.

If a file is corrupted, incomplete or not as described, contact us within 14 days and we will repair it, replace it or refund the purchase.

License

  • Personal: print for private use.
  • Commercial: sell physical prints, not the digital files.
  • Reselling, sharing or redistributing STL files is not allowed.
  • Each purchase is for one person or studio.

Privacy & payments

This site has no user accounts and no tracking cookies. Emails are used only to answer inquiries.

Payments are processed by third-party platforms. We do not receive or store card data.
